What is Dasha Sandhi? Dasha Sandhi is the transition window between two major planetary periods (Mahadashas). During this transition, energy shifts significantly, which can create uncertainty or adjustments in personal and marital life.
Key Timing Rule: Avoid finalizing wedding ceremonies during active peak Dasha Sandhi windows (especially intense transitions like Mars-Rahu, Ketu-Venus, Saturn-Mercury, or Moon-Mars).
